Keyboard & switch help

1–8 switch instruments on or off. Escape pauses the music. Tab moves through controls; Enter activates a button. In one-button mode, press Space or Enter to choose the highlighted action, without needing Tab. These keys work anywhere outside text fields and preference inputs. You can also tap the large gold button. The scan also includes Pause/Play, Clear band and Edit melodies. Inside the editor, the same gold button scans instruments, beats, notes and editing actions. A switch that sends Space works the same way. Use the slider to choose 1–8 seconds per choice. The scan pauses while this tab is hidden. Sound also pauses when you leave the tab, and waits for you to resume.
